## What it does

`RunEventWriteEos` on stage B no longer waits for the host copies and deferred signals before writing the
end-of-shader label. It runs `ProcessDownloadImages` and hands `SignalEos` to `SignalAfterHostCopies`. The signal is
recorded in stream order; when the Vulkan recording thread reaches it, `Gow3Copy::AfterCopies` writes the label once
every copy queued before it is done (on the recorder, or on the copy thread that finishes the last of them), as
end-of-pipe labels already were (RecorderFences). GDS stores keep the synchronous path: they read the GPU's data (`Finish`,
`ReadDataFromGds`). `GOW3_EOS_DEFERRED=0`, or `perf_gpu_data_no_waits=0`, restores the wait.

## Invariants

- The label reaches the guest only after the copies queued before it have run (the recorder's in-order execution
  plus `Gow3Copy::AfterCopies`). It may arrive later than before, never earlier. The guest and stage A's
  WAIT_REG_MEM must tolerate a late label; they do for the end-of-pipe ones.
- Labels stay in order: later guest-visible writes that are not deferred (WRITE_DATA, the flip) call
  `WaitDeferredSignals` first. Without that, a later value could overtake this label (`vk_scheduler.h`: freed memory
  written by the late fence, a corrupted heap).
- The deferral applies only on stage B (`DrawPipe::OnStageB()`) and with RecorderFences on (first word bit 41). On
  stage A, or with that bit set, the synchronous path runs.
- `GOW3_GPU_FENCES` (U5-2) is checked first and takes precedence. E1's branch is the copy model's path.
- The label is polled (no interrupt), so it is not urgent (`urgent = false`, added by recorder-lean's `ff54428`).
  With recorder lean mode it does not wake a sleeping recorder.
- The deferred signal only stores the label (`SignalEos`: `TryWriteBacking` or memcpy). It runs on the recorder or a
  copy thread, so it must never run guest code (W3b's deadlock).

## Interactions

- Needs lineage-recorder-fences (the deferred-signal machinery) and the two-stage pipe (stage B).
- W2 (caches): with the end-of-pipe readback wait targeted, this synchronous end-of-shader wait had taken the
  waiting over (T-S3). E1 is W2's companion; both are in `perf_gpu_data_no_waits`.
- recorder-lean: polled end-of-shader labels hand their chunk over without waking the recorder.
- W3 (rejected) tried the same deferral for WRITE_DATA flags.
- Not the Deck docs' "E1" (E1-deck-drains, stage A's drains). Same name, different patch.

## Evidence

- E1 + W2 is the unit (2026-11-07): T-S3 (neither) 0.52 -> T-E1 W2 off (E1 alone) 0.49-0.51 -> both 0.23 us a packet; without
  E1, W2 moved the wait into the end-of-shader fence (T-W2).
- No E1-only A/B exists. T-E1 (build `cb1da6c`, heavy view) had E1 on throughout and toggled W2 (bit 60, off for
  ~3 minutes live): ordered tasks 0.23 us a packet with W2 (end-of-shader 0.007) vs 0.49-0.51 without; ~372k vs
  ~351k packets/s (~5%). Those deltas are W2's. Against T-S3 (`ba9c433`, neither): ordered tasks 0.52-0.53, of which
  `RunEventWriteEos` 0.05; fix-inventory's "0.52 -> 0.23" is that E1 + W2 pair, not E1 alone. E1's own share is the
  end-of-shader time, 0.05 (T-S3, W2 off) -> 0.007 us a packet; with W2 alone the waiting moved into it (T-W2).
- The user (T-E1): "roughly the same ... There may have been a couple frame spikes", then "I don't think the spikes
  were real, I can't repeat them. 1% lows in light areas seem really good, I'm seeing 220 FPS with 200 1% lows".
  Kept on.
- What was left moved to `RunWriteData`'s `WaitDeferredSignals` (~1 ms once a frame), which led to W3.

## History and pitfalls

- The bottleneck after E1 was stage B's own draw work (1.6-1.7 us a packet).
- The Deck task split (2026-11-04) puts end-of-shader at 0.09 ms of a 13.6 ms heavy frame.
