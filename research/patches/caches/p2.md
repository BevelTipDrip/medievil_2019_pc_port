## What it does

With `GOW3_IDLE_EOP_SKIP=1`, an end of pipe in `ProcessDownloadImages` whose `download_images` is empty and whose
oldest queued readback is not ready (`pending_readbacks.FrontReady`) returns before the host-copy wait. Ends of pipe
with readback work still wait. `GOW3_TEXCACHE_STATS` counts both ("end of pipe with readback work (host copy wait)
..., without (skipped) ...").

## Invariants

- Only an end of pipe with no image to download and no readback ready to publish may skip; the check runs under
  `readback_mutex` + `download_images_mutex`.
- The guest's fence keeps its order without the wait: on stage B it is signalled by the recording thread after the
  copies (`SignalAfterHostCopies`); elsewhere `RunEventWriteEop` waits itself.
- Toggle bit 60 turns it off live (the same bit turns W2 off).

## Interactions

- superseded by W2: at an end of pipe P2 calls idle there is no image to download, and W2 waits only if a copy reads
  a queued readback's range (usually none; with nothing queued the list is empty); W2 also skips the wait at busy ends
  of pipe when no copy reads the ranges. With both on, P2's check runs first and returns before W2.
- C3: P2's worst frames were torches coming into view re-uploading a 64 + 32 MiB 4K target pair (T-R1), the cause
  C3 then removed.
- R1-stale-eop skipped the wait with readback work too and broke the torches; P2 kept that wait.

## Evidence

- P2a profile: ~20% of stage B spun in `ProcessDownloadImages` -> `WaitHostCopies` at every end of pipe.
- T-P2: 96% of ends of pipe skipped (menu 82,813 vs 3,753 per 5 s). Live A/B at the heavy spot: off 118-122 FPS,
  on 120-124 (~+2%); worst frame per window off <= 19 ms, on 28.7 / 31.0 / 41.8 ms at view transitions. The user:
  general performance and lows better outside the heavy area, stutter amplitude much higher; "the big stutters
  happen when a torch goes from not being in view to being in view". Made opt-in in `e385484`.

## History and pitfalls

- `e385484` also added the texture cache's re-upload report (top re-uploaded images by bytes), which found C3's
  cause in T-R1; that part belongs to texcache-stats.
- Rescored for the Deck (study-deck-rescore-rejected A12): ~0 expected, superseded. Retrying only makes sense with W2
  off; the 41.8 ms frame held 100.8 MB of image uploads, so its spikes would need re-measuring now that C3 is in.
