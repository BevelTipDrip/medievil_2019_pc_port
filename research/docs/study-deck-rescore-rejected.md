# Study: the stage B / CPU ideas never shipped, re-scored for the Steam Deck (2026-11-04)

Status: read-only research (docs, code, git log, Deck logs in `data/`). Nothing built, launched or changed.
Parent: [plan-linux-deck.md](plan-linux-deck.md) ("Native run 3: stage B by part", "E1 and the fix hit rates").
Companion: [study-stage-b-per-draw.md](study-stage-b-per-draw.md) (new per-draw cuts C1-C11; C1 built as `f03ee52`)
landed while this was written. Rows marked **[per-draw study]** overlap its scope; this page keeps only their history
and a Deck scale, and that study's ranking wins for them. Map: S1 here (C1) = its C4 + C7 + C8 together; S3 (C3) ~ its
C8; the bulk-copy extension and record skips (C4, C5) = its C4 and C7; own-target sets (C2) = its C10 (both: park);
W3 and the flip wait (A7, A1) = its C11; recorder spin (A13) = its C2; the Deck's 7.6x tasks = its section 3 (a
timeline-semaphore ioctl per end-of-pipe event, not CPU speed).

One count differs: its C10 / C3 read "~5" own-target and "~64 not memoizable" sets a frame by dividing the
"texture sets not kept" line by a 5-s window; that line prints every 262,144 render-state memo calls (~2.3 s here,
two per 5-s window: `deck-decode-D2.log:2376-2384`) and resets, so it is **~9-11 and ~115-140 a frame**. Both
conclusions stand (own-target small, "not memoizable" the big class).

Why (the user): "1. the prediction could be wrong; 2. it may not be a small gain on the Deck: what would be 0.2 ms on
my desktop could be 1 ms on the Deck."

Marks: **[V]** seen in a doc, log or the code (file:line given); **[I]** inferred (method given).

## The yardstick

- Deck heavy graveyard view after E1 (`c4a0147`): **64.5-64.9 FPS (~15.45 ms), stage B 91% busy**, stage A waits 30%
  all on the compute drain (plan-linux-deck.md:1035-1038) [V]. ~1,414 draws a frame (run 3, :676) [V].
  **1 us per draw = ~1.4 ms a frame**; while stage B is the wall, every ms it saves is ~4-7% FPS until the GPU or the
  game's own frame limits.
- Stage B "busy" includes its waits (flip, deferred signals, host copies: plan-stage-b-split.md:509-512) [V].
- Deck/PC per-draw ratios, run 3 (plan-linux-deck.md:679-695) [V]: packet 4.7, draw 4.2, bind 4.7, textures 4.7,
  ObtainBuffer 4.3, **vertex 5.4**, index 3.7, descriptors 3.7, texset 4.9, **texslow 6.3**, render state 3.4,
  begin render 3.0, obt_sync 5.3, **tasks 7.6, flip_present 6.5**. A PC estimate is scaled by the ratio of the section
  it cuts ("S:x4.2" below); threading ideas are judged by contention on 4 cores / 8 threads ("T"); "D" = the Deck's own
  number; "C" = a count from a Deck log times a scaled per-item cost.
- Contention on the Deck (plan-linux-deck.md:914-919) [V]: `gow3:DrawRec` ~1 core, `shadPS4:GpuComm` ~1 core
  (yield-spinning 53% of the time), the game's main thread ~1 core, `gow3:VkRecorder` 63% (before recorder lean `0771a23`; 2026-11-07 cuts redo: 41% uncapped at ~82 FPS, 28% at a 60 cap, so A2 and C13 below have even less to gain), Wwise 58%, `RHIThread` 42%,
  four `gow3:DrawPrep` ~19% each with ~1.7k involuntary switches/s each. There is no idle core; SCHED_IDLE workers
  still share a physical core with stage B through SMT.
- Every measured fix fires on the Deck as well as or better than on the PC (plan-linux-deck.md:1044-1048) [V]: the
  Deck does the same work, each piece 4-5x slower.

[V]. The toggle file works on the Deck (U2's live A/B, plan-linux-deck.md:972-973) [V]. Values: word 1 bit 19 =
`524288`, bit 34 = `17179869184`, bit 1 = `1` (`gpu/shim/gow3_toggles.h:25`, `:44`, `:47`) [V].
