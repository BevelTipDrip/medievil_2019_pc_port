$$ First-chance access violations are normal: the GPU page tracker write-protects guest pages and
$$ resolves the faults in its vectored handler (src/probe.c vectored_fault), many per second.
sxd av
$$ Illegal instructions: SSE4a emulation on Intel CPUs (src/runtime_sse4a.c); none on AMD.
sxd ii
$$ C++ exceptions thrown and caught inside drivers/libraries.
sxi eh
$$ A real fault: fatal_exception prints its report and calls TerminateProcess from inside the
$$ first-chance handler, so a second-chance break never comes. Stop at its entry instead:
$$ rcx = EXCEPTION_POINTERS* (ExceptionRecord at +0, ContextRecord at +8).
bp medievil_runtime!fatal_exception ".echo ==== medievil-runtime fault ====; .exr poi(@rcx); .cxr poi(@rcx+8); r; kv 40"
.echo MediEvil WinDbg setup loaded: av/ii first-chance ignored, break on fatal_exception.
